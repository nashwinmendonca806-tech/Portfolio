About

From CAD screen to tested hardware.

I’m Nashwin, a mechanical engineering student at Sardar Vallabhbhai National Institute of Technology (NIT Surat). I work where design, simulation and manufacturing meet: I model parts in SOLIDWORKS, optimize them in ANSYS, and 3D-print them to see how they really behave.

At DD Robocon 2026 I designed Team Drishti’s two cooperative robots and owned the fabrication of their 3D-printed parts. The team placed 4th nationally out of 130.

−78% structural weight

01 · Robotics · Topology optimization

Robotic Arm Structural Optimization

A robot arm engineered to be much lighter while keeping its stiffness.

What
Designed and fabricated a robotic arm with high structural stiffness and less material.
How
  • Initial CAD in SOLIDWORKS
  • Topology optimization in ANSYS
  • 3D printed the optimized parts
Results
  • Structural weight dropped by 78%
  • Structural stiffness dropped by only 18%
  • SOLIDWORKS
  • ANSYS
  • Topology optimization
  • 3D printing

02 · Mechanical design · Simulation · Testing

Custom Thrust Bearing

A custom 110 × 110 × 10 mm bearing, abuse-tested and designed around a 150-hour operating target.

What
Designed and abuse-tested a custom thrust bearing with a target operating time of 150 h.
How
  • ANSYS creep and static structural analysis
  • 3D printed the bearing guides
Results
  • Withstood an axial load of ~196 N
  • Only minute raceway deformation after 100 hours of operation
  • ANSYS
  • Creep analysis
  • Static structural
  • 3D printing
  • Abuse testing

04 · Competition robotics · Team Drishti

DD Robocon 2026

Two cooperative robots, designed, fabricated and integrated for a national robotics competition.

What
Designed 2 cooperative robots with 160+ components, 15+ assemblies and 5+ design iterations.
How
  • Owned fabrication of 60+ 3D-printed parts, tuning orientation, tolerances and print parameters
  • Applied GD&T to laser-cut components and gussets for accurate assembly fit
  • Applied DFM and creep analysis for reliable printed parts
  • Collaborated with the ECE team on mechanical–electrical integration
Results
  • 4th place nationally among 130 teams
  • Rubber wheel mechanism redesigned as a PETG thrust-bearing system, reducing friction, wobble and motor heating
  • Robotics
  • GD&T
  • DFM
  • 3D printing
  • PETG
